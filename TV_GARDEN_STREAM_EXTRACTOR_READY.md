## TV Garden Stream Extractor

Your TV Garden Stream Extractor is now ready! 

### Access the Stream Extractor

Visit: **http://localhost:3000/stream-extractor**

### What it does:

1. **Fetches 103 US news channels** with direct video stream URLs from TV Garden's GitHub repository
2. **Extracts M3U8 and YouTube URLs** for local use in your own video players  
3. **Provides copy-to-clipboard functionality** for easy URL access
4. **Filters by category** (news, sports, etc.) with search capability
5. **Opens YouTube streams directly** in a new tab when clicked

### Stream Types Available:

- **M3U8 IPTV Streams**: Direct HTTP Live Streaming URLs that work with VLC, IPTV players, and web video elements
- **YouTube Embed URLs**: Direct YouTube stream URLs that automatically open in YouTube

### How to Use:

1. Visit the stream extractor page above
2. Browse channels by category or use the search box
3. Click "Get Stream URL" to copy the direct video URL to your clipboard
4. Use the copied URL in your own video players, VLC, or streaming apps
5. For YouTube channels, it will also open the stream directly
